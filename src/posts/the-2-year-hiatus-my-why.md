It has been two years. Two whole years since I uploaded an episode of Tea Talks. If you were waiting for a new episode, you probably thought I died. Or got arrested. Or finally got a real job and deleted the internet from my brain.

Ngl, I kinda wish I did delete the internet from my brain sometimes. But I didn’t. I just ghosted. Hard.

I want to be clear about something. I didn’t stop podcasting because I was bored. I didn’t stop because I ran out of things to say. If you know me, you know I can yap for HOURS. The T in Tiana stands for Talkative. I could talk to a wall and make it interesting.

I stopped because I was in a deep coma. Not a medical one. A mental one.

**The Burnout Was Real (And It Took Everything)**

When I started Tea Talks, I was actually in a relationship. I wasn’t on medication. I wasn’t in therapy. I was just vibing. The podcast was an outlet. It was a place to dump all the tea and the trauma and the random thoughts that kept me up at 3 AM.

But then life happened. A lot of shit went down. The relationship ended. I started working at Sutter Health as a telecom operator. And suddenly, creating content felt like work.

Not fun work. Not "yapping with friends" work. Actual draining, soul-sucking work.

I had to find guests. I had to schedule recordings. I had to edit audio. I had to write show notes. I had to promote it. And while I was doing all that, I was also trying to stream. Well, "trying" is a strong word. I was streaming once every 3-4 months. Barely. Just enough to keep the account alive.

I was dealing with angry doctors on the phone all day. I was dealing with my own depression. I was dealing with the daily grind of being a human in California. And suddenly, hitting record felt impossible.

Something had to give. And it was the podcast. And mostly streaming too.

**The "Whole Lotta Nothing" Era**

During my hiatus I was doing a whole lotta something but at the same time a whole lotta nothing. Tbh I still am.

Not only was I freshly out of a relationship and just starting therapy and being in such a depressive hole, I was trying to navigate my new job, family life, trying to find myself again and find out how to have fun again. Honestly the single era didn't last long as I found a guy I was interested in a few months later and I decided to pursue him hard and we became official about 4 months later. Then I spent all of my time dedicating love and care into the relationship, and at least this time, I was getting it all back and more.

I was definitely doing a lot of other stuff though. I started vending at Pokemon card shows. I worked on new personal projects at home and at work. I was constantly planning get togethers with a large group of friends so I will continue to be a social butterfly. But all good things must come to an end. Luckily I am still with my amazing boyfriend, and I am still vending (kinda on a break rn for good reason) but the extra large friend group is no more and got replaced with a much smaller and closer knit group.

Then I decided fuck my job, imma do something else and quit my cushy job with great benefits and pay for going back to school. Not like getting my masters or something. I decided to go to trade school and be an esthetician. I started that January of this year, graduated, got my license and now I'm insuranceless, jobless, moneyless but licensed I guess. I have very very few clients and I make a little bit here and there but nothing to actually make me be able to live a cushy life. And I just turned 26 so I'm losing my insurance by the end of this month so I gotta find insurance fast or get married to my boyfriend asap!!!!

(Side note: Therapy didn’t start until about 2.5 years ago. Neither did the medication. So for a long time, I was just running on fumes and bad coping mechanisms. Fun times.)

**Why I’m Back Now**

So why now? Why after two years of silence am I dropping Season 2 Episode 1 with GodofPixelz?

Because I missed it.

I missed the feeling of hitting record and just letting go. I missed the feeling of having a deep conversation with a friend and knowing that other people were listening in. I missed the community. I missed the tea.

And honestly? I’m in a better place now. I’m on Prozac. It’s working better for me than Lexapro did. I’m not as numb. I’m not as anxious. I’m able to handle the stress of creating content without it destroying my mental health.

My current boyfriend definitely helped push me to bring it back. Some previous listeners kept asking where I went. And Pixelz was the final push I needed to actually hit record again. He reminded me that people actually like listening to us yap. But the rest? The tech scuff? The planning? The guest hunting? That’s all me. I am back on my grind of finding podcast guests and doing everything solo. Because I’m a masochist apparently.

**What To Expect From Season 2**

Season 2 is going to be different. I’m not going to promise weekly episodes. I’m not going to promise perfect audio. I’m not going to promise that I won’t go on tangents for 20 minutes about nothing.

But I am going to promise that it will be real. It will be raw. It will be unfiltered.

We are talking about mental health. We are talking about relationships. We are talking about the weird shit that happens when you try to be a creator in your 20s. We are talking about everything.

If you listened to Season 1, thank you. Thank you for sticking around even when I disappeared. Thank you for sending me messages asking if I was okay. Thank you for keeping the tea warm.

If you are new here, welcome. Pull up a cloud. The tea is hot. And I’m ready to spill it.

Listen to the new episode on Spotify, Apple Music, or YouTube. Link in bio.

And if you want to yell at me for taking so long, join the Discord. I can take it. I’ve dealt with a random caller telling me I’m a pedophile supporter at 8 AM (when in fact I’m not). I think I can handle you.

Lmaooooo.

Keep it real.

**The Guilt Trip**

For the first six months of the hiatus, I felt guilty. Every time I opened Spotify and saw my old episodes, I felt like a failure. I felt like I let my listeners down. I felt like I quit.

But then I realized something. I didn’t quit. I paused. There is a difference.

Quitting means you are done forever. Pausing means you are taking care of yourself so you can come back better. And I needed to pause. I needed to figure out who I was outside of being a content creator. I needed to figure out who I was outside of being a girlfriend. I needed to figure out who I was outside of being a customer service rep.

I needed to just be Tiana.
