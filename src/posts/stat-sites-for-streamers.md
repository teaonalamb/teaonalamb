Here are some helpful websites for live streamers to track and analyze their stats, offering insights into viewer engagement, growth, and performance across various platforms:

### 1. **TwitchTracker (twitchtracker.com)**
   - Provides detailed statistics for Twitch streamers, including viewer count, follower growth, stream duration, and peak times. It also offers insights into game popularity and overall platform trends.

### 2. **Social Blade (socialblade.com)**
   - Tracks analytics for multiple platforms, including Twitch, YouTube, and Instagram. It provides data on follower growth, view counts, and estimated earnings, with historical data to analyze long-term trends.

### 3. **SullyGnome (sullygnome.com)**
   - A Twitch-specific analytics tool that offers detailed stats on stream performance, including viewer counts, follower growth, stream hours, and game statistics. It’s useful for tracking both your own stats and those of other streamers.

### 4. **StreamElements (streamelements.com)**
   - Besides offering streaming tools, StreamElements provides in-depth analytics on viewer engagement, chat activity, follower growth, and revenue. It’s integrated with your streaming dashboard for real-time insights.

### 5. **TwitchMetrics (twitchmetrics.net)**
   - Another Twitch-focused analytics site that provides stats on viewer trends, follower growth, and game rankings. It’s useful for identifying the best times to stream and understanding your audience better.

### 6. **YouTube Studio (studio.youtube.com)**
   - The official dashboard for YouTube creators offers comprehensive analytics on video performance, including live stream stats. It tracks viewer retention, demographics, watch time, and revenue.

### 7. **Streamlabs Analytics (streamlabs.com)**
   - Offers performance metrics for streamers using Streamlabs. It provides data on viewer counts, engagement, and revenue, along with insights to help improve stream quality and viewer interaction.

### 8. **MixerStats (mixerstats.com)**
   - A site that provides detailed statistics for streamers on the Mixer platform. It offers data on viewership, engagement, and follower growth.

### 9. **VDO.Ninja Stats Dashboard (vdo.ninja)**
   - A tool that provides real-time streaming statistics, including bitrates, connection status, and stream health, which is especially useful for monitoring the technical aspects of your live streams.

### 10. **TwitchTools (twitchtools.com)**
   - Provides various Twitch-related tools, including stats on viewer counts, follower growth, and chat activity. It also offers tools like a Twitch chat analyzer to gain deeper insights into viewer engagement.

These websites are essential for live streamers looking to track their performance, understand audience behavior, and optimize their content for better engagement and growth.
