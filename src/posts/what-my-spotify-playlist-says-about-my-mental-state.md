Look, I’m not a therapist. I’m just a girl who needs to be the center of attention to feel alive, even though my brain chemistry is constantly trying to convince me to stay in bed and stare at the wall.

If you’ve been hanging around my Discord, listening to *Tea Talks*, or watching me struggle through games on Twitch, you know I don’t filter much. I talk fast. I talk loud. I love people. Being in a room full of friends, or even just having chat fly by at 100mph, is my fuel. I’m a complete extrovert. I get energy from chaos.

But here’s the twist: I’m also diagnosed with depression.

Yeah, the girl who loves being the life of the party is also the girl who has cycled through Lexapro, crashed hard on Wellbutrin, and is currently riding the Prozac train. I’ve been to therapy more times than I can count. I’ve sat in those chairs, spilled my guts, and tried to figure out why my brain feels like a browser with 50 tabs open and three of them are playing music.

So, if you really want to understand the dichotomy of teaonalamb—the loud streamer vs. the quiet depressive—don’t look at my bio. Look at my Spotify playlist. It’s a sonic representation of a Gen Z brain trying to balance serotonin levels with social battery.

Here’s the breakdown of what these tracks say about me, and probably you too.

### **The "Please Notice Me" Energy**

**Tracks:** *Boss Bitch* – Doja Cat, *Act Up* – City Girls, *Bodak Yellow* – Cardi B

Let’s start with the bangers. These aren’t just songs; they’re survival mechanisms.

As an extrovert, I thrive on attention. But it’s not just vanity. It’s validation. When I’m streaming or hosting a podcast, I’m feeding off the energy of the room. *Boss Bitch* isn’t just a vibe; it’s the armor I put on to step into that spotlight. It’s the song that tells my brain, "You belong here. You’re loud. You’re seen."

City Girls’ *Act Up*? That’s pure adrenaline. That’s the feeling of walking into a party and knowing you’re about to have the best night of your life. It’s the high I chase. Because when I’m "on," when I’m talking, laughing, and engaging, the depression feels miles away. It’s quieter. It’s manageable.

But here’s the catch: this energy is expensive. It burns hot and fast. And when the stream ends, and the room goes quiet, the crash is real.

### **The Chemical Crash (The Wellbutrin Week From Hell)**

**Tracks:** *Billie Jean* – EDEN, *deep coma* – haruno

Let’s keep it 100. Medication isn’t a magic fix. It’s a trial-and-error nightmare.

I was on Lexapro for a while. It worked. It numbed the edges. It let me function. Then life happened. A lot of shit went down. My doctor switched me to Wellbutrin (bupropion). If you know, you know. Wellbutrin isn’t an SSRI. It doesn’t block the reuptake of serotonin the same way. It’s supposed to give you energy. It’s supposed to help with focus.

For me? It was two weeks of absolute hell.

I cried every single day. Not pretty, cinematic tears. Ugly, snot-nosed, can’t-breathe tears. Because I was coming off the Lexapro shield, and the Wellbutrin wasn’t blocking enough. It wasn’t numbing enough. My brain was raw. Every emotion hit me at 100% volume. The sadness, the anxiety, the stress—it all came rushing in because the dam had broken, and the new wall wasn’t built yet.

*Billie Jean* by EDEN is the soundtrack to that week. It’s dark. It’s heavy. It’s the sound of lying on the floor wondering why your own brain feels like an enemy. *deep coma* by haruno? That’s the exhaustion. Not just physical tiredness. Soul tiredness.

This is the part of the playlist that reminds me that healing isn’t linear. Sometimes you take a step forward, and then you fall into a pit. And that’s okay. It’s part of the process. But fuck, it hurts.

### **The Safe Place (Joji, Cuco, and The Art of Dissociating)**

**Tracks:** *Ego Death In Thailand* – Cuco, *Glimpse of Us* – Joji, *C U Girl* – Steve Lacy

My safe places are Joji and Cuco. I have spent hundreds of dollars and have seen them multiple times. They have been on my Spotify Wrapped every year for the last 5 years. Their songs take me on so many emotional journeys, especially when I need it.

If you’ve never listened to *Ego Death In Thailand* by Cuco while staring at the ceiling at 3 AM, you haven’t truly lived. This song isn’t just a track; it’s a dissociation tool. It’s dreamy, hazy, and perfectly captures that feeling of wanting to float away from your problems. When the noise of being an extrovert gets too loud, when the pressure to perform gets too heavy, I put this on and I leave my body. I go to Thailand. I go anywhere but here.

And Joji? Joji is the king of sad boy hours. *Glimpse of Us* destroys me every time. It’s the sound of looking back at past relationships—the ones that didn’t work, the ones that taught me hard lessons—and realizing how far I’ve come, but also how much it still hurts.

These artists are my sanctuary. They don’t ask me to be loud. They don’t ask me to be "on." They let me be messy. They let me be sad. They validate the fact that I can be the life of the party one hour and a crying mess the next.

*C U Girl* by Steve Lacy fits here too. It’s the bridge between the chaos and the calm. It’s simple. It’s sweet. It’s the musical equivalent of sitting on the couch with my partner, not saying anything, just existing together. It grounds me. It reminds me that despite the chaos of multi-platform streaming and the pressure to always be "on," there’s still space for quiet moments.

### **The Chaos Agent (Because I’m Still 26)**

**Tracks:** *Cyber Sex* – Doja Cat, *Buss It* – Erica Banks, *CHAMPAGNE ROSÉ* – Quavo/Madonna/Cardi B

And then, just when you think I’ve figured it all out, the playlist throws *Cyber Sex* at you.

This is the reminder that I’m still figuring it out. I’ve been in many relationships. I’ve made mistakes. I’ve learned hard lessons. And sometimes, I just want to feel powerful and messy and unapologetic.

Doja Cat and Cardi B aren’t just music; they’re energy. They’re the part of me that refuses to be polite. The part that says, "Fuck this, I’m doing what I want." It’s necessary. If I only listened to Cuco, I’d never leave my house. If I only listened to EDEN, I’d be in a cave. I need the chaos. I need the bitchiness. I need the reminder that I can be both soft and sharp.

### **So, What Does It All Mean?**

My playlist is a mess because my life is a mess. And that’s okay.

I’m a lifelong Californian who loves the sun but hates the rent. I’m a former customer service rep who learned how to deal with people’s worst moments so I could handle my own. I’m a podcaster who took a break to heal. I’m a gamer who’s still trying to get better. I’m an extrovert who needs people to survive, but a depressed person who needs solitude to recharge.

My music reflects all of that. It’s anxious and confident. It’s soft and aggressive. It’s nostalgic and forward-looking. It’s chaotic.

If your playlist looks similar, you’re not alone. We’re all just trying to find the right song for the moment. Sometimes it’s a banger. Sometimes it’s a cry-in-the-shower ballad. Sometimes it’s the Backstreet Boys.

Check out the full playlist on Spotify if you want to vibe with the chaos. And if you want to talk about it, join the Discord. We’re always there, probably listening to the same stuff, trying to figure it out together.

Stay chaotic, stay soft, and keep the tea hot.
