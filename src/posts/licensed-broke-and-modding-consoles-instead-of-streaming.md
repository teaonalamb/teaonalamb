So it’s been a minute. Like actually a long minute. I haven’t streamed in forever. Haven’t touched my PC. The motivation just isn’t there right now and honestly? That’s okay. I’m not forcing it.

But I haven’t been doing nothing. I’ve been busy being a mess in new and exciting ways.

**The Esthetician Reality Check**

I am officially a licensed esthetician. Which sounds great on paper. In reality? I am basically flat out broke. My net worth is dropping every single month. I’ve been dipping into my savings just to pay off my credit card because I don’t have enough clients yet.

And the whole plan was to work part time for my mom. Take over the clients she doesn’t want. Quick easy money so I can start building something. But she keeps being back and forth about so much bullshit. Do I want to be an esthetician? Kinda. Do I want her to sell her business (which has been flourishing for a DECADE) to some random white woman? Hell no. So I have to do this. I have to make it work. But jesus christ I am so fucking poor. Genuinely poor. And with no stable income I can’t move out. And if I can’t move out Jose can’t move down. And Wang Wang can’t have a safe space away from my mom’s chaos. It’s a whole thing.

(Side note: I hate living in a house that isn’t a home. Why is that so hard?)

**Meds, Therapy, and Insurance Panic**

On the brain chemistry front things are actually kinda good? I got approved for Adderall for possible ADHD which is huge. And Prozac is still working. Lexapro made me numb. Wellbutrin made me cry every day for two weeks like a bitch. But Prozac? Prozac makes me feel relatively normal. Which is all I ask for really.

I might go back to therapy soon. Two different therapists probably. One for the ADHD assessment and back to my boi Jon to work through confronting people. But here’s the kicker: I lose my insurance at the end of November. So I gotta figure out Covered California or Medicare or whatever before then or I’m screwed. Can’t afford to lose the meds or the therapy rn. My brain needs them.

**Gaming Is My Therapy Now**

Since I’m not streaming I’ve been gaming for ME. Not for content. Just for sanity. I modded my 3DS. Modded my PS3. Modded my DSi. Bought an R4 card for my DS (didn’t need it tbh but whatever). Been playing on my Switch 2 exclusively for months. It’s healing. It’s quiet. It’s mine.

Streaming feels like work right now. Gaming feels like breathing.

**Wang Wang: The Spoiled Little Brat**

Let’s talk about the cat. Wang Wang. Adopted him August 7th when he was estimated to be 4 months old and 4.5 lbs. He’s a greyish brown tabby and he is SUPER dog-like. Super energetic. Loves to bite. Knows sit, paw, high five, roll over, and down. My mom is currently trying to teach him "pray" because she went from against having a cat to being absolutely obsessed with her grandson. Typical.

He’s almost 8.5 lbs now at roughly 7-8 months old. They say weight equals age in months so he might actually be older than we thought. Who knows. I like to say he was born April 7th (my anniversary with Jose) but then it’d be a double holiday and idk if I can handle that much celebration.

I’ve spent SO much money on him because he is a spoiled little brat. He’s super attached to me but thankfully has zero attachment issues. Which is good. But god I wish he was more cuddly. I just wanna hold him all the time and he’s like "nope, bite time." I love him. I hate him. When he passes away I am going to kms ngl. That’s just a fact.

**So Yeah. That’s Where I’m At.**

Licensed but broke. Medicated but anxious. Gaming but not streaming. Loving my biting cat but wanting to throw him out a window sometimes. Trying to figure out insurance before November hits like a truck.

It’s a lot. But it’s my lot. And I’m still here. Still yapping. Still figuring it out one modded console and one client at a time.

Stay chaotic. Stay soft. And keep the tea hot.
