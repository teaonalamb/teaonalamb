Finding the best deals on video games requires knowing where to look. I am always trying to save a buck here and there, especially with the hike in prices for games. Here are some of the best places and strategies to find great deals on video games:

### 1. **Online Retailers and Marketplaces**
- **Amazon**: Frequently offers discounts on physical and digital games, especially during sales events like Prime Day, Black Friday, and Cyber Monday.
- **Best Buy**: Regularly has sales on games, often including discounts on new releases. The "Deal of the Day" can sometimes feature games at significant discounts.
- **GameStop**: Offers new and used games, with frequent sales and promotions. Their trade-in program can also help reduce costs.
- **Walmart**: Known for competitive pricing on new and older titles, especially during holiday sales.
- **Target**: Often has "Buy One, Get One" (BOGO) deals or discounts on video games, particularly during large sales events.

### 2. **Digital Game Stores**
- **Steam**: The go-to platform for PC gamers, Steam’s Summer Sale, Winter Sale, and other seasonal events offer deep discounts on a wide variety of games.
- **Humble Bundle**: Offers bundles of games at pay-what-you-want prices, with a portion of the proceeds going to charity. Humble Store also has frequent sales on individual games.
- **GOG.com**: Specializes in DRM-free games and frequently has sales on both new and classic titles.
- **Epic Games Store**: Known for giving away free games regularly and offering significant discounts during sales events.
- **PlayStation Store**: Offers frequent sales and discounts on digital PlayStation games, especially during events like the "Big in Japan Sale" or the "Holiday Sale."
- **Xbox Store**: Similar to the PlayStation Store, with regular deals on Xbox games, particularly during the "Deals with Gold" for Xbox Live Gold members.
- **Nintendo eShop**: Occasionally runs sales on first-party and indie games, though discounts on first-party titles are less common.

### 3. **Price Comparison Websites**
- **IsThereAnyDeal.com**: Tracks game prices across multiple online stores and alerts you to the best current deals. You can set up price alerts for specific games.
- **CheapShark.com**: Another price comparison site that tracks game deals from various online stores.
- **GG.deals**: A comprehensive site for comparing game prices across multiple platforms and stores, with alerts for when prices drop to your desired level.

### 4. **Subscription Services**
- **Xbox Game Pass**: Offers access to a large library of games for a monthly fee. Often includes new releases on the first day.
- **PlayStation Plus Extra/Premium**: Provides access to a rotating selection of games each month, along with discounts on other purchases.
- **EA Play**: Grants access to a library of EA titles, along with early access trials and discounts on EA games.
- **Humble Choice**: A subscription service that gives you a selection of PC games each month at a discounted price.

### 5. **Bundle Deals**
- **Fanatical**: Regularly offers bundles of games at discounted prices. The "Star Deals" feature can also provide substantial savings on individual titles.
- **Green Man Gaming**: Offers discounts on digital game codes, with frequent sales events and "VIP" deals for members.

### 6. **Specialized Deal Websites and Forums**
- **Slickdeals.net**: A community-driven site where users share and vote on the best deals, including video games.
- **Reddit**: Subreddits like r/GameDeals and r/PatientGamers often highlight the best deals on games and gaming hardware.
- **HotUKDeals**: Particularly useful for those in the UK, this site aggregates deals across many retailers.

### 7. **Physical Store Sales**
- **Clearance Sections**: Check the clearance sections in stores like Walmart, Target, and Best Buy for heavily discounted games.
- **Local Game Stores**: Sometimes have sales, trade-in deals, or discounts on used games that can beat online prices.

### 8. **Cashback and Coupon Sites**
- **Rakuten**: Offers cashback on purchases made through their site, including games from major retailers.
- **Honey**: A browser extension that automatically applies coupon codes at checkout and tracks price history on items. (Currently still under hot water from scamming millions of dollars from thousands of content creators)

By combining these resources and keeping an eye on sales, you can consistently find the best deals on video games and save money on your purchases.(Unless you want to sail the seas for games for free ;))
