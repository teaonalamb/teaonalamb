Here are some websites that are particularly helpful for live streamers, offering a range of tools, resources, and communities to enhance your streaming experience:

### 1. **Twitch.tv**
   - The most popular platform for live streaming, especially for gaming. Twitch provides extensive resources, including tutorials, a Creator Dashboard, and a Partner/Affiliate program to monetize streams.

### 2. **YouTube Live**
   - YouTube's live streaming platform is integrated with its broader video services. It offers tools for live streaming, video archiving, and monetization through ads and memberships.

### 3. **Streamlabs (streamlabs.com)**
   - Offers an all-in-one streaming solution with free and premium tools, including Streamlabs OBS, a popular broadcasting software. It also provides alerts, overlays, donation management, and more.

### 4. **OBS Studio (obsproject.com)**
   - A free, open-source software for video recording and live streaming. OBS Studio is highly customizable and widely used by streamers for its powerful features and flexibility.

### 5. **Canva (canva.com)**
   - A graphic design platform that's great for creating stream overlays, banners, thumbnails, and other visual elements. Canva offers a range of templates specifically tailored for streamers.

### 6. **Nerd or Die (nerdordie.com)**
   - Provides premium and free overlays, alerts, and stream packages that can enhance the visual appeal of your stream. They also offer tutorials and other resources for streamers.

### 7. **Discord (discord.com)**
   - A communication platform used by many streamers to build communities and interact with viewers. Discord servers allow for text, voice, and video chat, making it an essential tool for community engagement.

### 8. **Restream.io**
   - A platform that allows you to broadcast your live stream to multiple platforms simultaneously, such as Twitch, YouTube, and Facebook. It’s great for reaching a wider audience.

### 9. **Lightstream (golightstream.com)**
   - A cloud-based streaming platform that simplifies the setup process and allows for easy customization of your stream. It’s particularly useful for console streamers and those new to streaming.

### 10. **Social Blade (socialblade.com)**
   - A website that provides analytics for YouTube, Twitch, Instagram, and more. Streamers can use it to track their growth, monitor competitors, and gain insights into their performance.

### 11. **StreamElements (streamelements.com)**
   - A full suite of streaming tools including overlays, alerts, and bots. StreamElements also offers powerful analytics to help you understand and grow your audience.

### 12. **Tiltify (tiltify.com)**
   - A fundraising platform specifically designed for live streamers. It integrates with your stream to allow viewers to donate to charities during live broadcasts, which can be a great way to engage your audience.

### 13. **Kapwing (kapwing.com)**
   - An online video editing tool that’s great for creating clips, highlights, and other video content to share on social media or upload to platforms like YouTube.

### 14. **Fiverr (fiverr.com)**
   - A marketplace where you can hire freelancers to create custom graphics, animations, music, or even provide streaming consultations. It’s a valuable resource for personalizing and improving your stream.

### 15. **Streamloots (streamloots.com)**
   - A platform that allows viewers to purchase cards that can trigger interactions during your live stream, adding an extra layer of engagement and monetization.

These websites provide essential tools, resources, and communities that can help you improve your live streaming setup, engage with your audience, and grow your channel.
