I mentioned in a past blog post that I went to literal dangerous lengths just to get podcast guests. Looking back, it was technically only twice, but still. Danger is danger, okay?

To give you some background context, my boyfriend of over two years broke up with me and I was depressed as hell. Like, so depressed my therapist prescribed me Lexapro. Woo, Lexapro! (Spoiler alert: I'm on Prozac now because holy shit, I gained so much weight on Lexapro and I was so emotionally numb it felt like I was floating through the void LMAO.)

To keep my spirits high, I was streaming on Twitch, gaming with my friends, and just chillin. But my viewership wasn't what it used to be, and seeing those numbers dip definitely hit my depression a bit too. So, I decided to hone in way more on my podcast. Podcasting is a great way for me to get stuff off my chest and just yap with people, which makes it super easy to forget about the real world for an hour.

The problem was, I did not want to have the exact same two or three friends on every single episode. I also didn't want to turn into a purely solo show either. I needed variety to make it more fun. Nowadays, I don't really care as much. I can do solo episodes or bring on repeat guests whenever. As long as I'm having fun and we hit about an hour, boom, it's an episode.

But back then? The only viable option my desperate brain could come up with to find new guests was... downloading dating apps.

Now, this wasn't my first rodeo with dating apps. I’ve actually been banned multiple times across multiple different platforms. Why? Usually because I had my Twitch or Insta in my bio, or I was promoting my Venmo to hustle a dollar or two LMAO. Also, I probably looked like a bot account because I’d paste the exact same copy-paste message to every guy who swiped right on me.

Since I've been on these apps before, I knew how to get swipes. I picked a few cute pictures of myself, along with a couple that were a little more softcore. You know, cleavage out, thighs showing, just enough to make sure guys actually stayed on my profile or swiped right. The catch? The *only* thing written in my bio was that I was a streamer, that I was looking for podcast guests, and that they should drop a follow.

Of course, the bio is below the photos, and a lot of guys just swipe right on literally every girl that pops up on their screen to maximize their matches. I actually know men have a way lower match rate because I tested it out before using photos of my ex-boyfriend (back when we were together) to make a Tinder profile just to see how the other side lives. I'm sciencey like that LMAO.

So yeah, I was totally feeding into the horrible stereotypes of dating profiles and essentially using my body to advertise my content. But it's not like I was posting straight-up porn or lingerie pictures! They were tasteful photos that left plenty to the imagination of lonely guys on the internet.

And naturally, the swipes flooded in.

I didn't stop at just one app either. I downloaded Tinder, Hinge, Bumble, Hily, Match.com, Coffee Meets Bagel, and like fifteen other random smaller apps. I literally went to the app store, downloaded the top 20 dating apps, and deployed the exact same strat across all of them.

Honestly, I kinda hated admitting this at first, but looking back, it's fucking hilarious. I don't do this anymore to advertise my socials because, to be real, the conversion rate is absolute garbage. All it did was get creepy dudes in my DMs saying "hey, check your DMs" or "saw you on Tinder." It is literally so embarrassing. I swear I don't whore myself out for clicks! (And just to be clear, I have zero judgment toward online sex work. If you're on Fansly or OnlyFans, get your bread! I support it 100%. I'd do it myself, but it's way too much work, I'm lazy, and I don't want my coochie out on the internet like that.)

I have a whole vault of screenshots from this era of my life with some of the funniest comments and messages imaginable. One guy messaged me, *"Are you a psyop? Because it's definitely working on me."* Another guy asked, *"Are you here just for content?"*

Like bro... yes! No shit! Why else do you think my entire bio is dedicated to my Twitch stream, links to my Instagram, and asking people to come record content for my podcast? Man, the literacy rate is really in the gutter LMAO.

My strategy was simple: go through all the likes I received, swipe back on every single one of them to build as large of a database pool as possible, and maximize my odds of landing a guest.

Predictably, most of the guys reached out because they just wanted to fuck and had zero interest in doing anything other than getting their dick tickled. Others expressed initial interest in being on the show, only to ghost me the second they realized I was dead serious about *only* doing a podcast episode and nothing more.

Out of hundreds of matches across 20 apps, I managed to get four people. FOUR. ONLY FOUR GUYS actually agreed to record, scheduled a date, committed to the show, and completed a full episode with me.

Here is where the reckless part comes in. For two of these strangers, I somehow decided, *"Yeah, sure, I'll record with you in person."*

I literally drove over to their apartments, completely alone, carrying recording gear, praying to God I wouldn't get murdered, raped, or kept captive in a basement. I put an absurd amount of trust in total strangers.

Fortunately, the first guy turned out to be totally normal and we just recorded in the common space on his apartment building's rooftop. The second guy, though? I was sitting dead-ass alone on his living room couch with him at like 9 PM. Both of these were recorded well after sunset. Why, past Tiana? Why?!

Honestly, the best part was that they supplied the beer. All I had to do was pay for the gas to drive over, set up my camera and mics, drink their free beer, yap in their living space for an hour, pack up my stuff, and leave, never to speak to them again. Honestly? Pretty big brain.

Part of me lowkey wants to do it again. (Well, more specifically, my friend Pixelz wants me to do it again because he wants us to share access to the dating profile accounts, and he mainly just wants to troll guys on a girl's dating profile LMAO this dude). But honestly, it's way too much work. I’d have to generate a new Google Voice number just to register new accounts, and I’d really rather not spend my free time risking my life for random podcast guests. Though, if Pixelz wants to man the account, troll guys, and somehow convince them to do a completely *online* podcast episode, hey... that works out LMAO!

Thankfully, I stayed safe and completely unharmed both times I met up with a stranger. I was around 23 when I did this, so my frontal lobe was not fully developed yet and I was running on a pure "fuck it we ball" mentality after my breakup. Looking back, I managed to pull off four episodes, get free beer, and escape without giving out any cooch or getting murdered. Honestly, I'm taking that as a win.

Moral of the story? Do not be fucking dumb like me. Don't go to random men's apartments late at night for content. But hey, I lived to tell the story, and I hope you guys still enjoyed those 4 episodes! :D
